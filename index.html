    System Overview

    MycoMonitor is an agricultural technology monitoring platform designed to oversee multi-room mushroom cultivation environments. It tracks temperature, relative humidity, and CO₂ concentration while managing cultivation batches, environmental analytics, threshold alerts, and ESP32 sensor nodes.
